Live editing and patch library over MIDI
InOut

A table plays a short list of waveforms and pitches at the start of a note, or loops it. This is how C64 drums and Rob Hubbard-style attacks are made. Set Table speed above Off or the table won't play; 1 is the fastest step rate, higher numbers are slower.

Press Receive bank, then on the unit: System › MIDI › SYSEXDump › AllPatch. A single Patch dump opens straight in the editor. Double-click a slot to edit it.
